Syntax reference

Hold’em

  • AA AKs AKo pairs, suited, offsuit
  • QQ+ ATs+ plus keeps the first rank fixed (ATs, AJs, AQs, AKs)
  • 76s-AKs A2s-A5s inclusive spans
  • AA@50% AA@.5 relative weight

Combining

  • , union: AA,KK
  • : intersection: KK:$ds
  • ! exclusion: AA!As; leading !AA is everything but AA
  • Precedence: , lowest, then !, then :. Use parentheses: (AA,KK):$ds

Omaha

  • AA$ds double suited (xxyy); $ss single suited (2+1+1); $r rainbow
  • $ts three of one suit; $ms four of one suit
  • $hs top card shares a suit; $as an ace shares a suit; $bs two top cards suited separately
  • Suit variables: xxyy 2+2, xxyz 2+1+1, xyzw four suits
  • Rank sets: [AKQ] or [K-T]; * wildcard
  • Two-card grids: {AA,KK|A2,A3} picks two cards from each side

Omaha AA includes any hand containing two aces plus anything (use AA!AAA to drop trips and quads). Not supported: top-N% rankings, 9876+ rundown shifts, (AK)(QJ) groups. Spell out rundowns: 9876,T987,JT98.
